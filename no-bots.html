Human players and server bots

Expanding Land arenas can contain human players and server-controlled bots. The in-game counters distinguish these populations. Counts change during play and differ by arena.

External automation

Server-controlled bots are distinct from automated clients operated by players. Check the current terms of service and server rules before using automation.

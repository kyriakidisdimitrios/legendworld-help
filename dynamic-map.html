Expanding Land Dynamic Map | Expansion and Contraction

Expanding Land uses a circular arena with a server-controlled border that can expand or contract during a session.

Expansion

When the arena grows, additional space becomes available. Use the minimap to orient yourself.

Contraction

When the border contracts, move toward the remaining playable area. Follow in-game warning and danger indicators.
